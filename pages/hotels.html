Hotels Ranked by Nearby MICHELIN Restaurants

Hotels in the MICHELIN Guide hotel selection, ranked by the number and star level of nearby MICHELIN-ranked restaurants. 2-adult per-night price estimates are before tax and fees.

How the ranking works

  • Hotels are sorted by the star value of nearby MICHELIN restaurants (3★ counts most, then 2★, then 1★, then Bib/Selected).
  • MICHELIN Keys are shown per hotel (1 = very special stay, 2 = exceptional stay, 3 = extraordinary stay).
  • Guest scores come from the MICHELIN Guide hotel page where available.
  • Price estimates are per night for 2 adults, before tax and fees — blank where unverified.
  • "Nearby" restaurants are those listed on the hotel’s own MICHELIN page under “Restaurants near the hotel.”

Baymich3 — Data verified against guide.michelin.com. Blank values = unverified, never inferred.
