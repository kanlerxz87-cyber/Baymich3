Verification Policy

  • No manual input, no hallucinations. Every value comes from an official source (guide.michelin.com or the restaurant/hotel’s own website).
  • If it can’t be confirmed: leave it blank. Blank values are flagged “unverified” — never inferred or filled in.
  • Every table row links to its official MICHELIN page. No row is without a source.
  • Star vs. listed is distinguished. A restaurant “listed in the guide” is not the same as one “currently holding a MICHELIN star.”

Answers to Common Questions

Q: Are the prices per person or for two adults?
A: Michelin price symbols ($–$$$$$) are per person. The “Est. 2 Adults” column is a derived range for two adults before tax, tip, and wine.
Q: What does “listed” mean vs “starred”?
A: “Starred” = currently holds 1, 2, or 3 MICHELIN stars. “Listed” = in the MICHELIN Guide (Bib Gourmand or Selected) but with no star. These are different statuses and are shown separately.
Q: How are hotels ranked?
A: By the star value of nearby MICHELIN restaurants listed on the hotel’s own Michelin page under “Restaurants near the hotel.”
Q: Is this site affiliated with MICHELIN?
A: No. Baymich3 is an independent compilation. All data is sourced from guide.michelin.com and linked back to the official pages.
