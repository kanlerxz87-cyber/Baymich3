Where the concentration is

San Francisco is the largest starred cluster in this working inventory. North Bay is a strong destination cluster because it contains multiple 3★ restaurants and winery-country hotels. This is cluster-level, not a measured-radius ranking.

Hotel choice

Use the hotel page as a shortlist only. Key levels, hotel addresses, weighted nearby restaurant counts, and actual driving/transit times need primary-source and geospatial validation before a final rank is defensible.

Booking priorities

For a high-end SF itinerary, compare 3★ options by availability, official menu total, dietary accommodations, and neighborhood. For value, compare Bib Gourmand options, but do not confuse Bib status with a star.

Important limitations

  • Data represents 2026 selection captured from official MICHELIN sources; restaurants can change status or close.
  • Many entry-level values (exact menu total, current hours, verified social accounts) are blank because source-level confirmation is not complete.
  • The Bib Gourmand list is explicitly partial and needs full official extraction.
  • Hotel ranking is a candidate shortlist, not a mathematically validated distance ranking.
  • Green Star transition status is irregular; preserve the edition date with the designation.

Before relying on this for booking, consult the restaurant's official reservation page and same-day hours. See Questions, Suggestions, and Flagged for Review.
