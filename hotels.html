What the cluster counts mean

  • What is counted: Michelin-starred restaurants assigned to each broad cluster (C1 25, C5 8, C2 5, C3 2, C4 1). These area totals are context only; they do not measure proximity to any individual hotel.
  • Not measured: straight-line or route distance, walk/transit/drive time, stay-date rates, or room value. No hotel ranking is defensible until the address set and method are verified and explicitly chosen.
  • Addresses: values are shown only when supported by the verification record and official source. Blank addresses are unverified, not inferred from nearby landmarks.
  • Candidate status: inclusion is not a Michelin hotel rating or booking recommendation. Rates and Michelin Key status have not been verified for every property.
