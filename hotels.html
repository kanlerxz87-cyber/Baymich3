Method, stated plainly

  • What is counted: starred restaurants in the hotel's cluster (C1 25, C5 8, C2 5, C3 2, C4 1). Ties are broken by how central the address is to the dense part of the cluster, which is a judgement, not a measurement.
  • What is not counted: nightly rates (dynamic, and a rate seen today is stale next week), drive times (not measured), and Michelin "Keys" unless the hotel's own site states them.
  • Address corrections found in this audit: Cavallo Point is at 601 Murray Circle, Fort Baker (not "601 Bridgeway"); 1 Hotel San Francisco is at 8 Mission Street (not "22 Battery"); Bardessono is at 6526 Yount St (the previously listed 6640 Washington St is The French Laundry's address).
  • Blank addresses mean the hotel's official page did not load or did not state a street address when checked, so nothing was filled in.

If you only read one line

For a first trip, stay in C1 SF Core — it holds 25 of the 41 Core starred restaurants and you can walk or ride-share to a different one each night. For an eating-first Wine Country trip, Auberge du Soleil and SingleThread Inn are the two places where the starred restaurant is in the building; Bardessono is the walk-to-French-Laundry option.

Ranking by density flatters the city. It says nothing about room quality, price or service — see Q6 and S5 before treating this as a booking list.
