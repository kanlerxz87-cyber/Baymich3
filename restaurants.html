What the price column includes

Total for two = (per-person menu price × 2) + the service charge the restaurant publishes. San Francisco venues add a 20–26% service charge plus, in some cases, a 4–6% city mandate, so the same menu price can land 20% apart depending on the operator. Sales tax and drinks are excluded. Where a restaurant publishes only "starting at", the row says so.

Only 17 of 44 rows currently have a publishable price. Most restaurants put prices behind the booking platform rather than on their own site; those rows stay blank until an official price is seen.
