Definitions (Michelin's own wording)

TierMeaningConfusable with a star?
Bib Gourmand"Good quality, good value cooking" — a personal recommendation for a good meal at a moderate price.No. It is not a star and never has been.
Selected / Recommended"Good cooking" — inspected and included in the Guide, with no star and no Bib.No. Inclusion only.
1 Star"High quality cooking, worth a stop."Yes — this is the first star tier.
