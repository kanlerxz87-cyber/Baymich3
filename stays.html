Suggested scoring model

  1. Set a radius and mode. Count open restaurants within a fixed walking radius (e.g. 1 km) and separately within an agreed transit or drive time. Do not mix them.
  2. Weight status transparently. Publish raw counts by one-, two-, three-star and Guide-listed (non-star) status; any weighted score is an optional secondary view.
  3. Compare actual stays. Quote hotel total for the exact nights, two adults, including mandatory taxes/fees; record the hotel’s own booking page and timestamp.
  4. Test the itinerary. Use route times to planned reservations, late-night return and transport cost; nearby restaurant density alone is not a hotel recommendation.

Area labels here are planning buckets, not claims about measured proximity or hotel quality. See open questions for required inputs.
