Evidence required to answer responsibly

Confirm the current Michelin selection and each venue's official page; collect official menu/pricing and hours; obtain trip dates; collect hotel official room quotes for those dates; geocode verified addresses; define “around” (for example, walking distance or drive time); then compute transparent counts and rank hotels using a disclosed scoring rule. Social media should be linked only after confirming it is the venue's official account.

Suggestions for review

Options to improve decision quality; no changes are assumed approved.

Suggested · highest impact

1. Establish scope and refresh date

Set geographic boundary (nine-county Bay Area vs. broader Northern California), include guide-selected/Bib/green categories or stars only, and refresh after Michelin's annual release. Label every status by edition.

Suggested

2. Add date-based cost model

Use two-adult menu totals from official menus; separately show tax, service, drinks, and mandatory fees only when official pricing confirms them. Request hotel quotes for selected dates and expose quote timestamps.

Suggested

3. Make the ranking explainable

Rank hotels by a chosen metric (count within an agreed travel-time radius, weighted stars, or budget-adjusted access). Show distances, transport mode, and tie-break rules. Avoid mixing “listed” with “starred.”

Suggested

4. Improve itinerary usability

Add filters for stars, price, cuisine, opening day, and transit access; provide an accessible map/list toggle; support save/share and reservation links; show last checked dates and flags for changing information.
