Current star picture

2026 edition

The official MICHELIN Guide California 2026 star rollup names 37 starred restaurants in this nine-county Bay Area scope, across the SF core, East Bay, Peninsula, Marin, and Wine Country.

There are 7 three-star, 7 two-star, and 23 one-star entries in the starred subset. A small sample of non-starred Guide-selected and Bib Gourmand restaurants is separated below; it is not a complete non-starred inventory.
