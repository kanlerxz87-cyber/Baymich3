What the numbers actually say

  • 41 starred in the Core Bay Area (7×3★, 7×2★, 27×1★). Adding the two Extension clusters (3 restaurants in Carmel and Elk) gives 44 starred rows in this dataset.
  • San Francisco city proper holds 25 starred restaurants — 4×3★, 6×2★, 15×1★ — roughly 61% of the Core list. Density is the story of this guide, not spread.
  • Wine Country is the second cluster with 8 starred restaurants, but it is a 60–90 minute drive from the city, so it behaves like a separate trip, not a suburb.
  • Verified dinner for two ranges $151–$1,037 before tax and drinks across the 17 rows where an official price exists. Most of the cheap end is lunch or a short menu; the top end is a full tasting menu plus service charge.
  • Honesty about gaps: 37 of 44 starred rows have at least one value verified on an official site, 1 is partial and 6 are unverified. Nothing has been filled in to look complete.

How to read a row

3 StarsHolds a star (3 = worth a special journey, 2 = worth a detour, 1 = worth a stop).
Bib Gourmand (no star)In the Guide for value — not a star, and not the same honour.
Selected / RecommendedIn the Guide, no star and no Bib. Shown by count and Michelin link, not enumerable from an official list yet.
VerifiedAt least one value on the row was read off the venue's own site or its Michelin page, and the link is on the row.
UnverifiedNot published on the official page we checked. Left blank on purpose.
$ for 2Per-person menu price × 2, plus the service charge/surcharge the venue publishes. Tax and drinks excluded unless stated. The formula is printed in each row.

Sources: MICHELIN Guide California 2026 (guide.michelin.com) and each venue's own website. Every row links to its source. Unverified means the value was not published on the official page we checked — it is left blank, never guessed. Data checked 24 Nov 2026.
