Executive Summary

Scope: San Francisco Bay Area MICHELIN Guide coverage — San Francisco, Oakland, Napa/Sonoma Valleys, San Jose area, Peninsula, Marin, and Wine Country.

Star vs. listed: This site clearly distinguishes restaurants that currently hold a MICHELIN star from those that are simply listed in the guide (Bib Gourmand or Selected). Every table row shows both the star level and the status.

Pricing: Michelin price symbols ($–$$$$$) are shown alongside a verified total price estimate range for two adults (before tax, tip, and wine). Where the estimate can’t be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified.

Social media: YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram links are shown beside each entry. Where none could be found on the official Michelin page or the restaurant’s own site, the slot is left blank (not inferred).

Hours: Days open, daily hours, and exceptions are shown per restaurant, taken directly from guide.michelin.com.

Sources: Every row links to its official MICHELIN Guide page. Nothing is invented.

Open Questions / Information / Answers

Q: Why are some prices blank?
A: Per user constraint, if a value can’t be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified — never inferred.
Q: Are social media links complete?
A: No. Michelin restaurant pages do not display YouTube/TikTok/Instagram links. Each entry shows blank social slots until verified from the restaurant’s own site or another official source.
Q: Enclos shows 3★ on its page but 2★ in the 2025 announcement.
A: Flagged as a discrepancy. The individual Michelin page renders three star icons; the 2025 ceremony article lists it as 2★. Needs re-verification after the next Michelin announcement.
Q: Is The Shota still open?
A: Some Michelin snippets note “temporarily closed.” Hours are shown (Wed–Sun 17:00–20:15) but status is unverified — call ahead.
