Map & clusters

The Bay Area's stars are not spread evenly. This page groups every starred restaurant into one of six clusters and links to the official Michelin pages that plot them. No coordinates are invented here — every map link goes to Michelin's or Google's own map, and addresses come from the Michelin listing or the venue's site.

Why no pins on this page: a hand-placed pin that is 300 metres off is worse than no pin, because it looks authoritative. Geocoding 44 verified addresses is proposed as S2 and needs your approval first.

Cluster logic in one paragraph

C1 is where a two-night trip works without a car. C3 is the value play: two starred rooms but a much deeper Bib bench, and BART connects it to C1. C5 is the only place with three-star cooking, and it deserves its own days. C2 and C4 are bases for specific meals rather than destinations. C6 is deliberately fenced off as an Extension so the Core counts stay honest.
