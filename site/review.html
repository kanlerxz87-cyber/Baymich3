EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · METHODS · GAPS

Make the next decision with eyes open.

Michelin’s live San Francisco-and-surroundings directory reports 145 restaurants in the captured result: 29 starred, 30 Bib Gourmand and 86 Selected. The 2026 California starred selection and official restaurant sites support the distinction and the price/schedule details shown. Many menus do not publish complete couple totals, and there is not yet a complete address-level dataset for hotel ranking. Those gaps are visible rather than estimated.

Suggestions to review before expanding

  1. Make the map the primary discovery tool. Add filters for current stars, Bib Gourmand, Selected, cuisine, price evidence, open day and geography.
  2. Publish two spending figures. “Menu subtotal for two” and “estimated all-in” should be separate; only calculate mandatory service fees when venue terms are explicit. Do not add discretionary tip, drinks or tax without documented amounts.
  3. Rank hotels transparently. Define search radius and travel mode, include all restaurant categories or state the selected subset, use verified venue addresses, and show both raw counts and coverage.
  4. Track freshness. Add checked-on date per source and a quarterly verification pass; Michelin distinctions can change annually and venue hours/prices change more often.
  5. Social links only from venue-owned pages. Do not link lookalike accounts or search-result accounts without official confirmation.
