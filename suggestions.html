Suggestions — review before choosing a path

This page separates proposed improvements from implemented functionality. These are options for a future review; no design choice is implied by listing them.

High impact · data quality

1. Complete the official-source audit

Extract every Bay Area 2026 Starred, Bib Gourmand, Recommended, and Green Star entry from MICHELIN. Verify city, current status, listing URL, official website, and checked date per row. Put uncertainties on the Flagged page instead of guessing.

Decision: include broader Monterey/Mendocino travel region or default nine-county Bay Area?

High impact · planning

2. Replace cluster-only hotel scores with travel-time analysis

Geocode verified addresses, calculate walk/transit/drive time bands (15/30/60 minutes), and score hotels on restaurants reachable by mode. Report 1★/2★/3★ and Bib counts separately, plus a no-car option.

Decision: optimize for number reachable, quality-weighted reach, or both?

High impact · budget

3. Standardize couple cost accounting

For each official menu, distinguish base price, mandatory service charge, tax, beverage pairing, and optional extras. Publish exact total only when current menu and required fees are explicit; otherwise blank with a reason.

Decision: default to food-only before tax or fully loaded meal estimate?

Useful · usability

4. Add filters and comparison controls

Filter by stars, city, cuisine, budget, open day, dietary accommodation, reservation lead time, transit access, and Green/Bib badge; add up to three restaurant comparison cards.

Useful · itinerary

5. Add sample itineraries

Build 1-day SF, 3-day city, 3-day wine-country, and coastal itineraries with realistic travel time, meal pacing, reservation links, and Bib alternatives. Clearly label assumptions.

Useful · media

6. Curate direct social profiles and clips

Use official restaurant social accounts and selected current YouTube/TikTok/Instagram content links. Include publisher, post date, and content type; avoid search links once direct sources are verified.

Suggested order

  1. Resolve scope/method defaults in the Questions page (agent can keep conservative defaults if no user input).
  2. Complete official selection inventory and validation.
  3. Verify prices/hours and social links.
  4. Geocode, calculate hotel reach, then finalize map/rank.
  5. Add advanced filters and itineraries only after core data quality is stable.
