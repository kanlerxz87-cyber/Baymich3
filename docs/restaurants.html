How to read the status

  • 3 Stars / 2 Stars / 1 Star: current Michelin star (from listing icons).
  • Bib Gourmand: good food at moderate price, not a star.
  • Listed (Selected): in the guide with no star or Bib.
  • Price tier: Michelin's $ to $$$$ scale (legend on the listing: $ on a budget, $$ moderate, $$$ special occasion, $$$$ spare no expense). It is not a price for two.
