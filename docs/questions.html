Questions, answers and gaps

What we know (with sources), and what must be answered before a decision.

Answered from official sources

How many starred restaurants are in San Francisco in 2026?25: 4 three-star, 6 two-star, 15 one-star.
Source: https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/guide-michelin-california
How many starred restaurants are in the core Bay Area (outside SF too)?33 total: 25 in SF plus Commis (Oakland, 2 stars), Sun Moon Studio (Oakland), Protégé (Palo Alto), Selby's (Atherton), Madcap (San Anselmo), Wakuriya (San Mateo), The Village Pub (Woodside), Plumed Horse (Saratoga) at one star each.
Source: https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/guide-michelin-california
How many Bib Gourmand restaurants are in the core Bay Area?36 on Michelin's official 2026 Bib list (addresses confirmed; 2 are new: Joodooboo, Kitchen Istanbul).
Source: https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/the-michelin-guide-california-2026
Which Bay Area restaurants are new in 2026?Californios (promoted to 3 stars), Naides (SF, new 1 star), Wolfsbane (SF, new 1 star), Joodooboo (Oakland, new Bib), Kitchen Istanbul (SF, new Bib).
Source: https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/guide-michelin-california
Does Michelin's list include the price for two adults?No. Michelin shows a price symbol ($ to $$$$) per restaurant, not a dollar amount. Exact prices must come from each restaurant's official menu.
Source: https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/benu

Still open (needed before a decision)

Exact price for 2 adults per restaurant (menu price, tax, service/tip, drinks)?Why it matters: Required by the brief. Needs official menu pages or booking pages for each restaurant. Not collected.
How to find it: Visit each restaurant's official website and booking page (links are on each row) and record the menu price, plus tax/tip assumptions.
Hours, days closed and holiday exceptions for every restaurant?Why it matters: Hours are verified for 20 restaurants only; Birdsong, Sons & Daughters, Atelier Crenn and some others are missing hours; holiday exceptions are not captured anywhere.
How to find it: Official restaurant websites; Google Business profile as a cross-check (not an official source).
Instagram, TikTok or YouTube links per restaurant?Why it matters: Required by the brief. Not researched yet.
How to find it: Social links from each official website footer; confirm handles on the platform itself.
Which hotels to rank, and by what rule?Why it matters: Ranking needs a hotel list (official source) and coordinates for each hotel and restaurant. Not collected.
How to find it: Michelin hotel selection pages and official hotel sites; choose a radius (e.g., 1 mile / 1.6 km) and a weighting (3-star = 3 points, 2-star = 2, 1-star = 1, Bib = 0.5) — decision needed (see Suggestions).
Should Michelin 'Recommended' restaurants (not starred, not Bib) be included?Why it matters: The brief says 'Michelin rated'. Statewide there are 323 Recommended entries; including them would greatly increase the table size and workload.
How to find it: Your decision. Options are listed in Suggestions.
What is the trip profile (dates, party size, budget, neighborhood preference, dietary needs)?Why it matters: Hotel ranking and any 'best value' ranking depend on it. The brief does not say.
How to find it: Your input.
Where should the live site be hosted and from which branch?Why it matters: Publishing to GitHub Pages from a feature branch vs. main changes what the public sees.
How to find it: Your decision.
Are the Michelin star counts consistent across all official sources?Why it matters: Third-party lists disagree in places (e.g., one site reports 3 three-star SF restaurants; Michelin lists 4). Michelin's own list was used, but the Michelin press PDF was not compared line-by-line with the article.
How to find it: Compare the official press PDF with the official article (already linked).
