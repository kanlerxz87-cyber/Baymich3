Executive summary

What the verified data shows, what it does not yet show, and what is needed before a decision.

Key facts (verified, official sources)

  • San Francisco & surroundings listing: 145 restaurants. Listing facets: 4 three-star, 7 two-star, 18 one-star, 30 Bib Gourmand, 86 Selected (official listing).
  • Three stars in SF: Quince, Californios, Benu, Atelier Crenn. Californios was promoted to three stars in 2026 (2026 release).
  • New 2026 one-stars in SF: Wolfsbane and Restaurant Naides (2026 release, and their official Michelin pages).
  • Sonoma: Enclos was promoted to three stars in 2026 (2026 release). Its address and price are not verified.
  • Bay Area Bib Gourmand (2026): 43 restaurants across 8 Bay Area counties, including new Bibs Kitchen Istanbul (SF) and Joodooboo (Oakland) (2026 release).
  • Hours verified: 7 restaurants (Quince, Californios, Benu, Atelier Crenn, Acquerello, Wolfsbane, Restaurant Naides).
  • Hotels: 18 of 31 captured. Among them, The Battery has Two MICHELIN Keys and Hotel Drisco has One MICHELIN Key (official index).

What is not yet known

  • Full star list: 1 two-star and 3 one-star entries are in the official facet counts but were not captured. Pages 2–4 of the listing were not fully read.
  • Price for two (total): not published on the Michelin pages checked. Only the $–$$$$ tier is official. No estimate is shown.
  • Social links: no official YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account was verified. Search helpers only.
  • Hotel proximity ranking: not computed. It needs verified coordinates or distances.
  • Map: not built. It needs verified coordinates for each restaurant and hotel.

Method

  • Official sources only: guide.michelin.com listing and detail pages, the michelin.com 2026 California press release, and the Michelin hotel index.
  • A value is shown only when read from one of those pages. Otherwise it is left blank and flagged.
  • Scope: "San Francisco and surroundings" listing for restaurants, the 9-county Bay Area for Bibs, Michelin's Bay Area hotel index.

Bottom line

Usable now: the star and Bib status of the listed restaurants, the 2026 changes, and official addresses for the Bibs. Not yet usable: any ranking of hotels by nearby restaurants, any price-for-two comparison, and any social-media recommendation. See Questions for what is needed and Suggestions for the next steps.

Every value is from an official Michelin page or the official 2026 Michelin press release. Blank or "Unverified" = not confirmed from an official source, left blank on purpose.
