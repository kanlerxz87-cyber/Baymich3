Key findings

  1. San Francisco is the cluster. 25 of 41 Bay Area starred restaurants are in San Francisco (60%). Other counties: Sonoma 4, Napa 4, San Mateo 3, Alameda 2, Santa Clara 2, Marin 1.
  2. Star changes in 2026. Three-star promotions: Californios, Enclos. New one-star entries: Naides, Troubadour, Wolfsbane.
  3. Star losses reported (secondary source only): Le Comptoir at Bar Crenn, O' by Claude Le Tohic, Shota. Needs review against Michelin before use.
  4. Hotels are not ranked yet. Ranking hotels by nearby stars needs verified hotel addresses and a distance method. See the Hotels page.
  5. Prices and social links are not yet verified. Michelin pages give only price tiers ($ to $$$$) for the restaurants checked so far, not exact prices for two adults. Exact prices and social links must come from restaurant menus and official accounts.
  6. Detail coverage is partial. Full Michelin detail (address, price tier, hours) was captured for 6 of 41 starred restaurants. The rest show only star status (from the official Michelin list).

Scope and method

  • Bay Area = nine Bay Area counties. Carmel-by-the-Sea (Monterey County) and Elk (Mendocino County) are excluded.
  • Star status comes from Michelin's official 2026 California article. Detail fields come from Michelin restaurant pages where fetched.
  • Bib and Recommended rows are from Michelin's official 2026 publication and article. Some Bib rows appear only in a secondary reproduction and are flagged.

What is still missing
