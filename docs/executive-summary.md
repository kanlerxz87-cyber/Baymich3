# Executive Summary — SF Bay Area Michelin Guide & Map (2026)

**Edition:** MICHELIN Guide California 2026 (revealed 24–26 June 2026). **Scope:** 9-county Core Bay Area + flagged Extension. **Checked:** 24 Nov 2026.

## Headline numbers (verified)
- **California 2026:** 83 starred (10×3★, 13×2★, 60×1★), 117 Bib Gourmand, 323 Recommended = 523 total. Source: Michelin Guide California 2026 article + Michelin press release.
- **Core Bay Area starred: 41** (7×3★, 7×2★, 27×1★). **Extension: 3** (Aubergine 2★, Harbor House 2★, Chez Noir 1★). Sacramento (2×1★) excluded as separate metro.
- **SF city proper:** 4×3★ (Atelier Crenn, Benu, Californios NEW, Quince), 6×2★ (Acquerello, Birdsong, Kiln, Lazy Bear, Saison, Sons & Daughters), 15×1★. Michelin "SF and surroundings" = 145 (29 starred + 30 Bib + 86 Selected).
- **Bib Gourmand Bay Area:** 36 rows in data/bib.csv, each now citing its own city's Michelin Bib list. Michelin's own filter counts are 30 (San Francisco and surroundings), 29 (Oakland and surroundings) and 28 (Berkeley and surroundings) and those lists overlap, so they cannot simply be added — a de-duplicated Bay Area total is still open (Questions Q8). 8 of the 36 rows were re-seen on a Michelin list page in this audit; the other 28 are flagged Unverified. **Recommended:** 86 in SF surroundings (Michelin count) — full names via Michelin search; this project lists Bib and Recommended by count + link (see Questions Q7).

## What changed in 2026 (decision-relevant)
- **Californios (SF) → 3★** — world's first 3-star Mexican restaurant. **Enclos (Sonoma) → 3★** (2★→3★ in one year). Both NEW 3★.
- **New 1★ in Bay Area:** Naides (SF, Filipino), Wolfsbane (SF), Troubadour (Healdsburg). Sun Moon Studio (Oakland) retained 1★ (new 2025).
- **Dropped from 2025 starred list (verify before booking):** Le Comptoir at Bar Crenn, O' by Claude Le Tohic, The Shota (was temporarily closed), Osito/Aphotic (closed), Chez TJ (now Recommended). See Flagged page.

## Clusters (where the stars are)
- **C1 SF Core:** 25 starred in city (densest). SoMa/Mission/Hayes Valley/Pacific Heights. Best for walkable multi-restaurant trips.
- **C2 Peninsula/South Bay:** 5 starred (Selby's, Protégé, Plumed Horse, Village Pub, Wakuriya). Best for Palo Alto/Stanford base.
- **C3 East Bay:** 2 starred (Commis 2★, Sun Moon 1★) + 10+ Bib (Oakland/Berkeley). Best for Piedmont/Berkeley base.
- **C4 Marin:** 1 starred (Madcap) + 3 Bib. Gateway, not a destination cluster.
- **C5 Wine Country (Napa/Sonoma):** **8 starred** — French Laundry 3★, SingleThread 3★, Enclos 3★, Auberge du Soleil 1★, Auro 1★, Cyrus 1★, Troubadour 1★, Press 1★. All eight are inside the 9-county Core (Napa and Sonoma), so all eight count. Cluster sum checks: 25 + 5 + 2 + 1 + 8 = 41. Best for restaurant-first itineraries (stay where you eat).
- **C6 Extension:** Carmel/Elk — trip add-ons, not base.

- **2★:** Acquerello $731.60 (295×2 +24%), Birdsong $700 (350×2), Commis $669.60 (279×2 +20%), Kiln $756 (315×2 +20%), Lazy Bear $640 (320×2), Sons & Daughters $660 (330×2), Aubergine $695.40 (285×2 +22%, Extension). Saison Unverified.
- **1★ (verified):** Angler lunch $151.20 (60×2 +26%), 7 Adams $320.04 (127×2, +6% surcharge +20% service), Chez Noir $396 (165×2 +20%, Extension), Sorrel $409.50 (195×2 +5% SF mandate), Troubadour $410 (205×2, Le Diner), Madcap $432 (180×2 +20%), Plumed Horse $450 (225×2), Ssal $540 (270×2), Auro $550 (275×2). The remaining 19 one-star rows are Unverified — see the table.
- **Rule:** Budget only from "Verified" rows. "Unverified" = check the official site or Tock before booking. Service charges of 20–26% are the single biggest swing factor — the same $195 menu costs $390 or $409.50 depending on how the operator publishes it.
- **Arithmetic corrections made in this audit:** Acquerello was shown as $708 (basis $731.60), Angler as $156 (basis $151.20), Commis as $669 (basis $669.60), 7 Adams as $254 (omitted both surcharges), Sorrel as $390 (summer menu), Madcap as $360 (omitted 20%). All now match their printed formula.
