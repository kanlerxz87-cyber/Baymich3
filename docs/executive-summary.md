# Executive Summary — SF Bay Area Michelin Guide & Map (2026)

**Edition:** MICHELIN Guide California 2026 (revealed 24–26 June 2026).
**Scope:** 9-county Core Bay Area (41 starred) + Extension outside the 9 counties (3 starred).
**Last checked:** 24 Nov 2026 (session 15c870c6). Every number below links to a source in the Sources section.

## 1. Headline numbers (verified)
- **Statewide, 2026:** 83 starred (10×3★, 13×2★, 60×1★). Source: [Michelin California 2026 article](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/guide-michelin-california).
- **Core Bay Area starred: 41** = 7×3★, 7×2★, 27×1★. Checked row by row against the Michelin article's star lists (Bay Area towns only).
- **Extension (outside 9 counties): 3** = Aubergine 2★ (Carmel), Harbor House Inn 2★ (Elk), Chez Noir 1★ (Carmel). Sacramento's 2 starred restaurants are excluded.
- **SF city proper:** 25 starred = 4×3★ (Atelier Crenn, Benu, Californios, Quince), 6×2★, 15×1★.
- **Michelin's own "San Francisco and surroundings" filter** lists 145 restaurants: 29 starred, 30 Bib Gourmand, 86 Selected. That area is narrower than our 9-county Core, so the counts differ. Source: [Michelin SF restaurants](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurants).
