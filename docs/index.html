Key points

  • The official MICHELIN Guide California 2026 selection (announced 24 June 2026) lists 83 starred restaurants statewide: 10 three-star, 13 two-star, 60 one-star (Michelin article).
  • San Francisco has 25 starred restaurants: 4 three-star (Atelier Crenn, Benu, Californios, Quince), 6 two-star, 15 one-star (Michelin article).
  • Core SF Bay Area (SF, East Bay, Peninsula, South Bay, Marin) has 33 starred restaurants and 36 Bib Gourmand restaurants in the official 2026 lists. Wine Country (Napa/Sonoma) adds 8 starred restaurants, tracked separately.
  • Bay Area 2026 changes: Californios promoted to 3 stars; Naides and Wolfsbane are new one-star restaurants; Joodooboo and Kitchen Istanbul are new Bib Gourmands. A third-party report also lists four Bay Area restaurants losing stars (Le Comptoir at Bar Crenn, O' by Claude Le Tohic, The Shota, plus Kenzo in Napa); Michelin's own losses list was not confirmed.
  • Verification status: 20 restaurants have full verification from their Michelin restaurant page (address, cuisine, price tier, official website, and most hours). The other 13 starred and 36 Bib rows are verified only at the level of the official Michelin list (star or Bib status, and address for Bib rows). Those rows are flagged.
  • Not yet done: exact 2-adult prices (no official menu prices collected), social media links, hotel ranking, and the rest of the restaurant detail pages. These fields are left blank on purpose rather than estimated.
  • Hotel ranking (task T3) is not started. Michelin's own hotel selection for California names SingleThread Inn (3 Keys, Healdsburg) and Bernardus Lodge & Spa (2 Keys, Carmel Valley); no Bay Area ranking has been produced.
