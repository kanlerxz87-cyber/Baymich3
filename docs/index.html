How to use this site

  • Michelin stars lists each starred restaurant, its star level, whether it is new or promoted, and its source.
  • Bib & Recommended lists the value-focused Bib Gourmand and other Michelin recommendations.
  • Executive summary has the key takeaways.
  • Questions lists what must be answered before a decision can be made.
  • Suggestions lists improvements for you to approve or reject.
  • Flagged for review lists repo irregularities and conflicting data.
