Flagged for review

Irregularities found while checking this repository line by line, in the order they were found. Nothing here is hidden behind a status badge: each item states what was wrong, what was done, and what is still open.

1. Column corruption in the starred data fixed

What was wrong: eleven rows (Naides, Wolfsbane, Sun Moon Studio, Auro, Troubadour, Press, Plumed Horse, The Village Pub, Aubergine, Chez Noir and Hilda and Jesse's neighbour row) had the Michelin URL stored in the price_pp_usd column. Every later field had shifted one place, so the rendered page printed a URL where a price belonged and showed no address, no hours and no sources. A twelfth row (Lazy Bear) had its Instagram link in the YouTube column.

What was done: all 44 rows were rebuilt in canonical 24-column order, the shift was reversed per row, and a build-time assertion now fails if any row lacks a Michelin source link. The generator reads the CSV, so the HTML cannot drift out of step again.

2. Price arithmetic fixed

  • Acquerello displayed $708 for two while its own stated basis was $731.60 ($295 × 2, +24%). Corrected to $731.60; the surcharge is 20% service plus a 4% employee-benefit surcharge.
  • Angler displayed $156 against a $151.20 basis ($60 × 2, +26%). Corrected to $151.20.
  • Commis displayed $669 against $669.60. Corrected.
  • 7 Adams omitted its charges entirely. Its own menu page states a 6% surcharge and a 20% service charge, so the total for two is $320.04, not $254. Both figures now appear with the formula.
  • Sorrel had a summer price in a Fall 2026 dataset. Corrected to the Fall menu ($195 pp) with the 5% SF mandate added: $409.50 for two.
  • Madcap omitted the 20% service charge it publishes in place of gratuity: $432 for two.

3. Wrong hotel addresses fixed

  • Bardessono was listed at 6640 Washington St — that is The French Laundry's address. Bardessono's own contact page gives 6526 Yount St, Yountville.
  • Cavallo Point was listed at "601 Bridgeway". Its contact page gives 601 Murray Circle, Fort Baker, Sausalito.
  • 1 Hotel San Francisco was listed at 22 Battery St. Its own site gives 8 Mission St.

Five further hotels (St. Regis, Palace, Ritz-Carlton, Taj Campton Place, Rosewood Sand Hill, Claremont, Hotel Kabuki) have addresses that could not be confirmed from an official page during this audit. Their address cells are now blank and labelled Unverified rather than carrying a plausible-looking guess.

4. Source provenance for Bib Gourmand fixed

What was wrong: every Bib row, including Oakland, Berkeley, Marin and Wine Country restaurants, cited the San Francisco Bib Gourmand list as its source, and all 36 were labelled "verified". Michelin publishes separate city lists, so the citation was wrong even where the restaurant was genuinely a Bib.

What was done: each row now cites its own city's list, and rows whose names were not actually re-seen on a Michelin list page in this audit are marked Unverified (28 of 36). Michelin's own filter counts are 30 for San Francisco, 29 for Oakland and 28 for Berkeley, and those lists overlap — so a simple sum would also have been wrong.

5. Cluster count contradiction fixed

The executive summary described Wine Country as having 7 starred restaurants while listing 8 names (The French Laundry, SingleThread, Enclos, Auberge du Soleil, Auro, Cyrus, Troubadour, Press). The data has 8, and the sum across clusters is 25 + 5 + 2 + 1 + 8 = 41, matching the Core total. The text now says 8 and all cluster counts are computed from the CSV at build time.

6. Sources that could not be read open

These are recorded as Unverified rather than filled from memory:

  • frenchlaundry.com returned HTTP 500 on every attempt, so The French Laundry has no price and no hours.
  • pressnapavalley.com/food returned a bot challenge. Press has verified hours from its homepage but no price.
  • enclos-sonoma.com is a video-only page with no readable text, so Enclos has no hours or price.
  • statebirdsf.com and theprogress-sf.com publish reservation links only, so both rows are Unverified for price and hours.
  • californiossf.com publishes address, phone and a booking link, but no price and no hours. Californios, the first 3-star Mexican restaurant in the world, therefore has no price on this site.
  • Mister Jiu's, San Ho Won, Protege, Wakuriya and Saison publish menus as PDFs or images. The prices are real but were not machine-readable, so they stay blank.

Discovery route for each is on the Questions page (Q3, Q4).

7. Editorial notes worth a human eye review

  • Selby's city label: Michelin files it under Atherton, the street address is in Redwood City. Both are shown.
  • Cyrus address: Michelin prints "587 St. Helena St." for Press, which is inconsistent with the restaurant's own "587 St Helena Highway". The official form is used.
  • Auberge du Soleil: it appears both as a restaurant (1 star) and as a hotel. It is counted once in restaurant totals and once in hotel rankings, deliberately.
  • Extension vs Core: Aubergine, Harbor House Inn and Chez Noir are starred but sit in Monterey and Mendocino counties. They are excluded from the Core count of 41 and shown separately; folding them in would inflate the Bay Area figure.
  • Recommended tier: 86 names are counted but not enumerated, because Michelin does not publish that set as one page. This is a stated gap, not an oversight.
