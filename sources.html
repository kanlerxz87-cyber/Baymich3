Scope defaults

“Bay Area” in this project means SF, East Bay, Peninsula/South Bay, and northern destination clusters in Sonoma/Napa, plus Monterey/Carmel and Mendocino coast for a broad visitor itinerary. Because this extends beyond the conventional nine-county Bay Area, site pages label region/cluster and the standard boundary remains an open question. Sacramento entries are explicitly excluded from Bay Area totals.

Status terminology: Starred = 1–3 MICHELIN stars; Bib Gourmand = separate value distinction, no star implied; Green Star = separate sustainability distinction that may overlap with stars; Recommended = listed in the Guide without a star (not fully inventoried on this initial site). Guide status is edition-specific.
